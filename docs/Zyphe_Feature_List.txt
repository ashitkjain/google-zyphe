Zyphe: Technical Specification and Feature Set

This document outlines the functional capabilities and technical features of the Zyphe platform.

1. Geospatial and Neighborhood Analysis
Zyphe utilizes multi-source spatial data and computer vision to analyze property environments.
Visual Neighborhood Identification: Processed analysis of Google Street View imagery to identify:
Privacy Assessment: Evaluation of natural screening, fencing, and visibility from public thoroughfares.
External Asset Condition: Detection of visible maintenance indicators including roof integrity, driveway condition, and exterior finish.
Logistics Mapping: Identification of garage configurations, driveway capacity, and street parking regulations.
Safety Infrastructure: Detection of sidewalk continuity and perimeter fencing.
Utility Infrastructure Mapping: Identification of overhead power lines and utility pole presence.
Environmental Monitoring:
Air Quality Metrics: Real-time reporting of Universal Air Quality Index (UAQI) and specific pollutant concentrations (CO, NO2, PM2.5, PM10).
Pollen and Allergen Analysis: Data on local pollen types, concentration levels, and seasonal trends.
Solar Visibility Analysis: Assessment of solar potential based on historical sunshine data and 3D obstruction detection (neighboring structures and vegetation).

2. Computer Vision Listing Analysis
Automated processing of listing photographs to extract structural and design data.
Interior Design Classification: Categorization of interior styles based on visual cues (e.g., Japandi, Modern, Traditional).
Finish and Material Extraction: Identification of flooring types (hardwood, tile, carpet) and surface materials.
Room Classification: Automated tagging and analysis of specific areas (Kitchens, Living Rooms, Primary Suites).
Image Metadata Audit: Evaluation of photo quality parameters including lighting, composition, and staging for listing optimization.

3. Workflow and Productivity Suite
Operational tools for lead management and transaction tracking.
Kanban Transaction Pipeline: A visual management system for tracking lead status and transaction progression.
Universal Lead Ingestion: API support for capturing leads from third-party portals, social media channels, and existing CRM databases.
Database Reactivation Logic: Automated analysis of historical lead data to identify re-engagement opportunities based on new search activity.
Integrated Scheduling: A calendar system where scheduled events are linked to specific property data records and analysis reports.

4. Financial and Market Analysis
Quantitative tools for investment assessment and offer strategy.
Bidding Range Analysis: Calculation of suggested offer ranges based on local Days on Market (DOM) and recent comparable sales.
Rental Market Projections:
Short-Term Rental (STR): Projected occupancy rates and Average Daily Rate (ADR) estimations.
Long-Term Rental (LTR): Projected monthly rent and vacancy rate analysis.
Comparable Property Integration: Retrieval and display of recent similar property transactions in the immediate vicinity.

5. Risk and Mobility Intelligence
Climate Risk Modeling: Risk indexing for environmental hazards including flood, fire, wind, and heat.
Mobility Scores: Integration of Walk, Transit, and Bike scores.
Education Mapping: Identification of nearby educational institutions with associated rating data.
Structural Fact Extraction: Automated extraction of building specifications (foundation type, sewer system, utility configuration).

6. Persistence and Architecture
Cloud Data Caching: Persistence of all environmental and visual analyses in a centralized database for cross-platform retrieval.
System Deployment Options: Supports both a comprehensive standalone platform and modular API-based integration with external real estate software (kvCORE, HubSpot, Chime).
